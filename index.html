Add GiftPass to your Home Screen

It opens like a normal app, full screen, and works without signal at the till.

  1. Tap the Share buttonAt the bottom of Safari. On iOS 26, tap ••• first, then Share.
  2. Choose “Add to Home Screen”Scroll down the menu to find it. On iOS 26 it may be under View More.
  3. Tap “Add”Leave Open as Web App on if you see it. GiftPass is now on your Home Screen.
  1. Tap the ⋮ menuIn the top-right corner of Chrome.
  2. Tap “Add to home screen”It may say Install app instead.
  3. Tap “Install”GiftPass is added to your home screen and app drawer.

Then open GiftPass from your Home Screen and add your first card. Cards added in the browser stay in the browser, so add the app first.
